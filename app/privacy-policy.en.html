Privacy Policy

ZenVR - Immersive Meditation

Last updated: November 2024

1. Introduction

ZenVR ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use our virtual reality meditation application.

2. Information We Collect

ZenVR does NOT collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

Specifically:

  • ❌ We do NOT collect name, email, or contact information
  • ❌ We do NOT collect geographical location
  • ❌ We do NOT collect usage history
  • ❌ We do NOT collect health or wellness data
  • ❌ We do NOT use tracking cookies
  • ❌ We do NOT share data with third parties

3. Application Permissions

ZenVR requests the following device permissions:

  • Motion Sensors (Gyroscope/Accelerometer): Required for head tracking in VR mode
  • Local Storage: Used exclusively for offline caching of content (via Service Worker)
  • Internet: To load meditation videos and audio (first time only)

ℹ️ All permissions are used exclusively for app functionality, with zero data collection.

4. Data Storage

ZenVR functions as a Progressive Web App (PWA) and stores content locally solely for:

  • Caching videos, audio, and images for offline use
  • Interface preference settings (if implemented)

All data remains exclusively on your device and can be deleted at any time through your browser settings or by uninstalling the application.

5. Third-Party Services

ZenVR may use third-party services for basic functionality:

  • Google Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (if implemented)
  • CDN (Tailwind CSS): Externally hosted style libraries

ℹ️ These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to consult them.

6. Security

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no risk of sensitive information leakage. All content is delivered over HTTPS to guarantee transmission security.

7. Minors

ZenVR is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU) because we do not collect data from anyone.

8. Advertising

ZenVR is completely free and does NOT display advertisements.

9. Your Rights (LGPD/GDPR)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data to:

  • Access
  • Correct
  • Delete
  • Export
  • Restrict processing

You have complete control over cached data on your device through your browser settings.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the application. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
